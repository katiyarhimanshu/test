As per UPTU curricular we are planning to organize a seminar series from 22nd March 2011. For that all the EC31 & EC32 students have to prepare a seminar reports strictly in the format of IEEE conference format (single spacing double column) and should not exceed more than five pages. Please write the contents of your seminar topic in your own language because seminar reports will be checked with the help of anti-plagiarism tools. Marks will be awarded strictly on the basis of the content of seminar report, formatting and presentation. Please put your seminar report in a strip file and submit it to the faculty members during the time of presentation. Before the presentation, seminar report is required to be sent electronically via E-mail: katiyarhimanshu@rediffmail.com. Maximum allocated time for the presentation is 10 minutes so prepare your slides accordingly. Students are advised to give the presentation in their allocated slot else presentation will be shifted to the next available slot along with a penalty of 10 marks.
